what am i building?

  • Project Fermat — a volunteer initiative teaching olympiad maths to middle schoolers in underserved schools across Delhi NCR. notes and problem sets are released free, so the work reaches students we can't teach in person
  • dynamic-island-macos — a native macOS Dynamic Island in Swift that lives in the notch. shows Spotify off the app's own broadcast rather than polling, mirrors a real folder through FSEvents so there's never a second copy of the state, and runs an on-device voice assistant. the part i'm proud of is a performance fix: rewriting the observation graph and moving thumbnail decoding off the main thread took median CPU from about 15% to about 4%
  • avellaneda-stoikov-sim — a Python limit order book simulator for studying market making, inventory risk and adverse selection. four strategies including Avellaneda–Stoikov and GLFT, with fill intensity calibrated from simulated order flow rather than assumed
  • monte-carlo-quant-lab — Monte Carlo methods in quantitative finance, written from scratch. option pricing, exotics, variance reduction, VaR/CVaR, all checked against Black–Scholes and other closed forms
  • Reckon — a screen time app built on habit formation mechanics rather than willpower
  • this website — plain HTML and one stylesheet, no framework and no build step
